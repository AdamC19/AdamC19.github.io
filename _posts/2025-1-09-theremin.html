Basic Oscillator Building Block

The classic theremin uses a heterodyne architecture to generate the pitch of the tone. In simple terms, you make two nearly identical osciallators. The LO will hopefully be pretty constant. The pitch oscillator involves the pitch plate in its feedback loop. The pitch oscillator output will drop in frequency with the player's hand brought closer to the pitch antenna. The LO and the Pitch tone are fed into a frequency mixer, which is a nifty building block that allows you to do addition and subtraction between signals in the frequency domain.
